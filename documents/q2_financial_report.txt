AEGIS ENTERPRISE
Q2 2026 FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE REPORT

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Enterprise revenue declined to $645,000 in Q2 2026, compared with $860,000 in Q1 2026.

The decline was concentrated among large enterprise accounts and the Aegis Cloud product line.

REGIONAL PERFORMANCE

North America generated $390,000 in revenue.

EMEA generated $170,000 in revenue.

APAC generated $85,000 in revenue.

The largest regional decline occurred in North America, primarily because Titan Technologies reduced its cloud spending.

PRODUCT PERFORMANCE

Aegis Cloud generated $270,000 in Q2, compared with $380,000 in Q1.

Aegis Analytics generated $275,000.

Aegis Security generated $100,000.

CUSTOMER PERFORMANCE

Titan Technologies reduced spending from $200,000 in Q1 to $130,000 in Q2.

Vertex Financial reduced spending from $180,000 to $140,000.

Nova Healthcare reduced spending from $160,000 to $100,000.

Apex Industries reduced spending from $150,000 to $120,000.

KEY RISKS

Enterprise customers increased scrutiny of cloud infrastructure spending.

Several customers delayed planned expansion projects.

Sales cycles increased due to additional procurement reviews.
