EXECUTIVE STRATEGY MEMO
Q2 2026

TO: Executive Leadership Team
FROM: Chief Strategy Office

The Q2 revenue decline appears to be driven by a combination of customer budget pressure and delayed cloud expansion initiatives.

Large enterprise customers have introduced stricter approval requirements for infrastructure and software spending.

The sales organization reports that procurement cycles increased by approximately 20 percent compared with Q1.

Titan Technologies delayed a major cloud expansion project.

Vertex Financial postponed a planned infrastructure modernization initiative.

Leadership recommends focusing on existing customer retention, usage expansion opportunities, and smaller phased deployments.
