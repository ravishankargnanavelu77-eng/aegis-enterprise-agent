CUSTOMER FEEDBACK SUMMARY
Q2 2026

Enterprise customers reported increased pressure to reduce discretionary technology spending.

Common themes included:

- Longer internal approval processes.
- Cloud cost optimization initiatives.
- Preference for phased implementation instead of large deployments.
- Requests for improved pricing flexibility.

Titan Technologies stated that its infrastructure budget was reduced during Q2.

Vertex Financial requested postponement of a major expansion due to internal budget reviews.

Nova Healthcare indicated that it was prioritizing regulatory technology investments over infrastructure expansion.

Customers generally expressed satisfaction with existing Aegis products, indicating that the revenue decline was primarily related to spending constraints rather than product dissatisfaction.
