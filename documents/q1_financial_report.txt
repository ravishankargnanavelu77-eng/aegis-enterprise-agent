AEGIS ENTERPRISE
Q1 2026 FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE REPORT

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Enterprise revenue in Q1 2026 reached $860,000. Revenue performance was strong across North America and EMEA, driven primarily by Aegis Cloud and Aegis Analytics.

REGIONAL PERFORMANCE

North America generated $530,000 in revenue. Major contributors included Vertex Financial, Titan Technologies, and Apex Industries.

EMEA generated $240,000 in revenue. Nova Healthcare and BlueSky Logistics were the primary contributors.

APAC generated $90,000 in revenue, primarily from Orion Retail Group.

PRODUCT PERFORMANCE

Aegis Cloud generated $380,000 in revenue.

Aegis Analytics generated $320,000 in revenue.

Aegis Security generated $160,000 in revenue.

CUSTOMER PERFORMANCE

Titan Technologies was the largest enterprise customer with $200,000 in revenue.

Vertex Financial contributed $180,000.

Nova Healthcare contributed $160,000.

Apex Industries contributed $150,000.

OUTLOOK

The company entered Q2 with a positive enterprise sales pipeline and expectations of continued cloud adoption.
