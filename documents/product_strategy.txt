AEGIS PRODUCT STRATEGY
2026

Aegis Cloud remains the company's largest strategic growth area.

The Q2 decline in cloud revenue requires increased focus on:

1. Usage optimization features.
2. Flexible enterprise pricing.
3. Incremental deployment models.
4. Customer retention analytics.

The product organization is developing a cost optimization dashboard to help enterprise customers reduce unnecessary infrastructure spending.

The strategy team expects demand for cloud services to recover as enterprise technology budgets stabilize.
